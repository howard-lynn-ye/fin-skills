fin_skills.tax_accounting.ashare_taxes

A-share taxes: a seller-side stamp duty that halved in 2023, and a dividend tax that charges you for turnover.

WHY this exists: almost every A-share backtest prices costs as a flat symmetric number -- "10 bps a side" -- and the two taxes that actually apply are neither flat nor symmetric.

STAMP DUTY (证券交易印花税) is levied on the SELLER ONLY. 印花税法 Article 3, verbatim: "证券交易印花税对证券交易的出让方征收，不对受让方征收。" A buy pays zero. And the rate HALVED on 2023-08-28, so a constant rate is wrong on one side of that date whichever number you pick.

DIVIDEND TAX (股息红利个人所得税) is a STEP FUNCTION OF HOLDING PERIOD: exempt over one year, an effective 10% from one month to one year, an effective 20% at one month or less. A dividend-capture strategy and a buy-and-hold strategy collect the same cash and keep different amounts of it. That is a turnover penalty written into the tax code, and no A-share backtester models it.

Usage:

from ashare_taxes import stamp_duty, dividend_tax_rate, compare_cost_models stamp_duty(1_000_000, side="S", date="2023-08-28") -> 500.0 stamp_duty(1_000_000, side="B", date="2023-08-28") -> 0.0

These are MODELLING assumptions for a backtest, not tax advice, and the rules below are for an INDIVIDUAL resident investor. Institutions, funds, QFII/RQFII and Stock Connect investors are all treated differently and none of that is here. Check current law with a professional before relying on any of it.

SOURCES (read 2026-09-09): 中华人民共和国印花税法 (in force from 2022-07-01), fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100009/ c5193058/content.html -- Article 3 defines 证券交易 as the transfer of shares and share-based depositary receipts traded on a lawfully established exchange, and levies the duty on the transferor only; the attached 印花税税目税率表 sets the rate at 1 per mille of the transaction amount (成交金额). 财政部 税务总局公告2023年第39号《关于减半征收证券交易印花税的公告》, issued 2023-08-27, fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c102416/c5211343/content.html -- "为活跃资本市场、提振投资者 信心，自2023年8月28日起，证券交易印花税实施减半征收。" 1 per mille x 50% = 0.05%. 财政部 news release 2008-09-19, mof.gov.cn/zhengwuxinxi/caizhengxinwen/200809/ t20080919_76432.htm -- "调整为单边征税，即对买卖、继承、赠与所书立的A股、B股股权转让书据 的出让方按千分之一的税率征收证券（股票）交易印花税，对受让方不再征税。" The document number is reported elsewhere as 财税明电〔2008〕2号; that number is secondhand. 财税〔2015〕101号《财政部 国家税务总局 证监会关于上市公司股息红利差别化个人所得税政策有关 问题的通知》, issued 2015-09-07, effective 2015-09-08, chinatax.gov.cn/n810341/n810755/c1797427/content.html -- >1 year exempt; <=1 month full inclusion at 20%; >1 month to <=1 year 50% inclusion at 20%. Withholding is deferred: the company withholds nothing at payment and 中国结算 computes it when the shares are sold. Article 4 (the NEEQ paragraph) was repealed by 公告2019年第78号; the dividend rules above remain in force and were applied by reference again in 公告2026年第8号 (2026-01-14).

def holding_bucket(buy_date, sell_date) -> str:

财税〔2015〕101号: '<=1m', '1m-1y' or '>1y'. Calendar months, not 30 days.

The boundaries are inclusive on the low side: 持股期限在1个月以内(含1个月) is the 20% bucket, and 1个月以上至1年(含1年) is the 10% bucket, so exactly one year is still taxed.
