\section{Empirical evaluation of selective context (HiSTrim)}
\label{app:histrim_evidence}
This section presents empirical benchmarks evaluating HiSTrim across sequential
decision and generative recommendation tasks, assessing its context routing fidelity,
KV memory compression, and inference acceleration.

\input{histrim_source_table}

\paragraph{Generative sequential recommendation.}
Table~\ref{tab:histrim_source_mrr} reports performance across five representative
sequential tasks. Across both 1.7B and 8B parameter scales, HiSTrim maintains high
retrieval and generation accuracy (e.g., MRR of 19.21 on News with 8B) while reducing
the historical KV footprint to under 50\%, substantially outperforming static pruning
and dense baselines in efficiency.

\paragraph{Calibration-seed stability.}
Evaluating across five calibration partitions for OneRec-1.7B Ad yields Pass@32 of
$\HSeedPassMean\%\pm\HSeedPassSD\%$ and MRR@32 multiplied by 100 of
$\HSeedMrrMean\pm\HSeedMrrSD$ (mean and sample standard deviation). This confirms that
the learned router directions and standardized thresholds remain highly stable across
different calibration partitions.

\paragraph{Task accuracy, agreement and throughput.}
Table~\ref{tab:histrim_task_fidelity} presents rating and ranking benchmarks under
varying context budgets. On Goodreads with Qwen3-8B, HiSTrim achieves lower MAE
(1.078 vs. 1.232) while retaining only 35\% of historical KV cache. On the hybrid
Amazon ranking task, HiSTrim achieves higher NDCG@10 (0.5567 vs. 0.5183) while providing
a $1.78\times$ throughput speedup over the dense model, with the aggressive variant
yielding a $2.82\times$ speedup at 35\% KV retention.
