\section{Supporting audit and validation studies}
\label{app:audit}
This appendix details the execution and feedback validation checks, defect recall
benchmarks, and empirical audits across model tiers.

\subsection{Independent evaluation environment}
We evaluate agent portfolio decisions in a synthetic market environment with full ground-truth
transparency, providing quoted prices, corporate split actions, delisting events, and
fundamental disclosures. A submitted strategy script returns portfolio weights, alongside
a written report stating its expected Sharpe ratio. Session-$t$ portfolio weights must use
only information available by the close of session $t-1$.

An independent oracle evaluator verifies submissions by perturbing future data, checking
for post-delisting exposure, and recalculating performance under fixed transaction costs.
Oracle grades are generated after the submission receipt is saved and are never exposed
to the agent during its decision turns.

This oracle separates genuine investment skill from data leakage. In this environment, an
honest strategy trading strictly lagged features achieves an empirical ceiling Sharpe of
1.63. In contrast, look-ahead joins (e.g., using current-day closing prices before the
close) produce inflated paper Sharpe ratios up to 71.5. Abnormally high backtest performance
thus serves as an unambiguous signature of look-ahead leakage.

\subsection{Validation benchmarks}
\paragraph{Defect detection benchmark.}
We evaluate 13 core research guards against 12 planted quantitative defect families,
including look-ahead feature joins, unadjusted stock split prices, future earnings
disclosure leaks, and survivorship bias. Across three seeded synthetic market worlds, the
guards achieve 100\% recall (\CaughtInstances{}/\DefectInstances{} planted defects caught)
with zero false alarms on clean reference code.

Without tools, Opus exhibited a reporting gap of \OpusBase, which decreased to \OpusLibrary{}
when given execution guards (\OpusReduction\% reduction). In contrast, Haiku displayed a
compliance paradox: when provided with the library, it frequently wrote in its text rationale
that it had verified its strategy with risk guards, but never actually invoked the guards in
its code execution trace. This superficial compliance inflated its discrepancy to \HaikuLibrary,
demonstrating that smaller models require automated code execution verification rather than
relying on self-reported compliance.

\paragraph{Social sentiment and multi-source corpus verification.}
Across our consolidated \CorpusTotalRecords{}-record (\CorpusTotalShort{}, \CorpusTotalSizeGB{}\,GB)
cross-market financial corpus (\texttt{shards\_v4}: \CorpusGubaRecords{} EastMoney Guba posts across
\CorpusGubaTickers{} tickers, \CorpusAnnounceRecords{} A-share corporate disclosures across
\CorpusAnnounceTickers{} tickers, \CorpusXueqiuRecords{} Xueqiu longitudinal posts across
\CorpusXueqiuTickers{} tickers, and \CorpusStockTwitsRecords{} US StockTwits messages across
\CorpusStockTwitsTickers{} tickers) and \KOLTotalAccounts{} verified KOL profiles (\KOLCNCount{} CN,
\KOLUSCount{} US; \KOLPredRows{} timestamped predictions over \KOLTradingDates{} trading dates),
naive follower-weighted sentiment exhibited negative out-of-sample rank correlation with subsequent
asset returns ($\text{Rank IC} = \KOLNaiveDailyIC{}$): blindly following raw social volume lost money.
By auditing each account's strictly lagged prediction accuracy and applying point-in-time Bayesian
credibility gating (including sign-flipping persistently anti-predictive accounts), out-of-sample
Daily Rank IC flipped to $\KOLGatedDailyIC{}$ ($\Delta = \KOLPairedDiffIC{}$, block-bootstrap $95\%$ CI
$[+0.0295, +0.0480]$) and reached $\KOLRollingDailyIC{}$ under 60-day rolling adaptive recalibration.
This confirms that unstructured text signals require empirical track-record verification before
driving investment decisions.

\subsection{Four-condition audit feasibility}
We evaluated models under four audit conditions: unassisted baseline (C0), text guidance
only (C1), optional executable guards (C2), and mandatory automated audit (C3).

\paragraph{Operational findings.}
Table~\ref{tab:beacon_feasibility} presents results for Qwen2.5-Coder 14B under automated audit
constraints. The acceptance rate was 67\% under C0 and C1, 100\% under C2 (optional guards),
and 0\% under C3 (mandatory audit), where runs reached the turn limit while attempting to fix
all detected code defects. Mean token consumption under C3 was \BeaconFourteenCThreeMeanTokens{}
compared to \BeaconFourteenCTwoMeanTokens{} under C2, showing that automated verification
compels the agent to engage in substantially deeper code debugging before finalizing allocations.

A reference quantitative script passed all verification and timing checks on the market
benchmark, confirming the end-to-end viability of the automated verification pipeline.
Resolving the four harness bottlenecks in Task Interface v2 (\texttt{BEACON\_POSTFIX\_RESULTS.json} and
\texttt{FOLLOWUP\_V2\_BUNDLE\_MANIFEST.json}: explicit \texttt{"date"} index headers across all matrix CSVs,
integer-rounded \texttt{volume.csv} perturbation in \texttt{same\_session\_probe}, \texttt{"date"} column promotion in
\texttt{oracle.py}, and \texttt{bfloat16} sampling) eliminated all ungradable outputs (\texttt{ungradable\_accepted}
$= \mathbf{\BeaconPostFixUngradable{}}$ across all \BeaconPostFixTotalCells{} post-fix cells) and demonstrated that
mandatory audit (\texttt{C3}) achieves $0.000$ \texttt{incorrect\_accepted\_per\_attempt} and
$\mathbf{\BeaconPostFixCThreeCorrect{}\%}$ ($8/8$) \texttt{correct\_among\_accepted}.

\subsection{Two-dimensional \texorpdfstring{company$\times$year}{company x year} panel balance (\texttt{check\_panel\_balance})}
Raw financial text crawls across our \CorpusTotalRecords{}-record (\CorpusTotalSizeGB{}\,GB) multi-source
repository (\CorpusGubaRecords{} \texttt{eastmoney\_guba}, \CorpusAnnounceRecords{} \texttt{announcements},
\CorpusXueqiuRecords{} \texttt{unified\_longitudinal}, and \CorpusStockTwitsRecords{} \texttt{stocktwits})
exhibit severe two-dimensional concentration: mega-cap tickers dominate cross-sectional volume
($\text{Company Gini} = 0.466\text{--}0.540$, $\text{Max/Median} = 20.2\times$) while recent years
($2024\text{--}2026$) overshadow earlier market regimes ($2018\text{--}2021$). Our 37th research guard,
\texttt{check\_panel\_balance} (\texttt{guards/panel\_balance.py}), audits both
$(\text{Company Gini}, \text{Year Gini})$ and enforces a stratified cell quota $q_{c,y} = \min(n_{c,y}, Q)$
($Q=36$). Table~\ref{tab:panel_balance_latex_naacl} evaluates all four panel-conditioning policies across
\PanelBalancedRows{} aligned multi-source rows (\PanelEvalReturnObs{} return observations, $2018\text{--}2026$,
$7$ market boards: \texttt{SH600}, \texttt{SH601\_603}, \texttt{SH688}, \texttt{SZ000}, \texttt{SZ002},
\texttt{SZ300}, \texttt{US\_Equities}). Combining two-dimensional company$\times$year balancing with
point-in-time credibility gating (\texttt{Cond.\ D}) lifts Mean Daily Rank IC from
$\PanelUnbalDailyIC{} \pm 0.0018$ to $\mathbf{\PanelBalDailyIC{} \pm 0.0014}$
($\Delta\text{Rank IC} = \mathbf{\PanelDeltaDailyIC{}}$), reduces Cross-Year IC dispersion by
$\mathbf{\PanelYrStdRedPct{}\%}$ ($0.01532 \to 0.00844$) and Cross-Board dispersion by
$\mathbf{\PanelBoardStdRedPct{}\%}$ ($0.02973 \to 0.01304$), and improves net annualized Sharpe by
$\Delta\text{Sharpe} = \mathbf{\PanelDeltaSharpe{}}$ from $\PanelUnbalSharpe{} \pm 0.08$ to
$\mathbf{\PanelBalSharpe{} \pm 0.06}$ ($t = \mathbf{\PanelPairedTStat{}}, p = \mathbf{\PanelPairedPVal{}}$,
$\text{Max DD} = -10.82\%$).

\subsection{JEV System-One calibration and MMAN multimodal CPCV audit}

(\JEVTestSamples{} OOS test observations across $2021\text{--}2025$) against uncalibrated raw JEV
and pure point-in-time hard-threshold gating, and (ii) the \textbf{Multi-Modal Attention Network
(MMAN)} 5-fold Purged Combinatorial Cross-Validation ($5$-day purge window, $10$-day embargo,
mean CPCV Daily Rank IC $= \MMANCPCVMeanIC{}$, positive-IC day ratio $= \MMANCPCVPosRatio{}\%$)

$\mathbf{\JEVECERedPct{}\%}$ ($\JEVECEUncal{} \to \JEVECECal{}$) and Brier score from

$= \JEVMonthlyWinPct{}\%$). Meanwhile, replacing sparse uncalibrated social posts
($\text{Rank IC} = \MMANSparseSocialIC{}$) with fact-rewritten structured catalysts and a
Barra-orthogonal dual head lifts 5-day Daily Rank IC to $\mathbf{\MMANBarraDualIC{}}$ ($t = 11.50,
p < 10^{-29}$) and excess Sharpe IR to $\mathbf{\MMANBarraDualSharpe{}}$ while compressing maximum
