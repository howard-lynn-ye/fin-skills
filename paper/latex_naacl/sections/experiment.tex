Table~\ref{tab:calibration} proves that static year holdouts (\texttt{2023}) fail to punish same-session leakage ($22.80$ paper Sharpe vs.\ $-0.58$ live replay). Evaluating \texttt{FinGuardBench-60} (\path{run_finguard_bench_60.py}, \path{run_extended_ablations_e9_e12.py}) across four conditions, four model tiers, and five feedback levels (Table~\ref{tab:pilot}, Fig.~\ref{fig:overview}a, Appendix~\ref{app:e5}--\ref{app:e12}) yields three insights:
\textbf{(1) Python \texttt{Self-Debug} \& Feedback Granularity (E5, E9):} Reading \texttt{SKILL.md} (\texttt{Cond B}) lifts compliance from $30.0\%$ to $70.0\%$ ($p=1.19\times 10^{-7}$). Adding 3 rounds of Python \texttt{Self-Debug} \citep{chen2023selfdebug} (\texttt{Cond B+}) stagnates at $71.7\%$ ($p=1.0$) as $59/60$ ($98.3\%$) of financial leaks exit with code $0$. Ablating guard feedback granularity (Fig.~\ref{fig:e9_e10}a, Table~\ref{tab:e9_feedback}) shows that binary rejection (\texttt{FAIL} only) reaches $80.0\%$ \texttt{Pass@3} and guard-name-only reaches $88.3\%$, whereas full structured \texttt{GuardResult} row/timestamp counterfactuals (\texttt{Cond C}) achieve $\mathbf{98.3\%}$ ($p=0.00098$ vs.\ binary), cutting $|\Delta\text{Sharpe}|$ from $1.04 \to 0.04$.
\textbf{(2) Cross-Model Paradox \& Progressive Disclosure (E6, E12):} Under \texttt{Cond B}, hallucinated guard citations drop from $65.0\%$ (\texttt{Small-Fast}) and $53.3\%$ (\texttt{Open-Coder}) to $28.3\%$ (\texttt{Sonnet/4o}) and $8.3\%$ (\texttt{Opus/R1}), while \texttt{Cond C} enforces $\mathbf{0.0\%}$ hallucinations across all tiers (Fig.~\ref{fig:domain_scaling}b). Moreover, our progressive disclosure index ($7.21\text{k}$ tokens/task) reduces prompt tokens by $\mathbf{95.0\%}$ ($5.4\times$ faster) while beating $142.8\text{k}$-token full-library stuffing ($98.3\%$ vs.\ $88.3\%$ \texttt{Pass@3}, Table~\ref{tab:e12_delivery}) by avoiding long-context attention dilution.

\paragraph{Universal Follower Asymmetry \& Rolling Bayesian Reversal.}
Table~\ref{tab:kol} and Fig.~\ref{fig:overview}b confirm that Social Megaphone Bias holds across both languages: contrarian indicators command \textbf{$3.96\times$ more followers in China ($55{,}989$ vs.\ $14{,}122$, $\text{IC} = -0.2130$)} and \textbf{$3.15\times$ more in the US ($58{,}120$ vs.\ $18{,}450$, $\text{IC} = -0.1764$)} than Tier-1 Alpha researchers. Unweighted sentiment suffers negative correlation in both markets ($\text{IC} = -0.03184$ CN, $-0.02184$ US, Table~\ref{tab:mman}). Our \texttt{kol-\allowbreak credibility-\allowbreak registry} reverses Rank IC to $+0.01040$ (CN) and $+0.00942$ (US); furthermore, 60-day rolling Bayesian updates prevent the $49.1\%$ prior decay of static frozen weights ($+0.0101$ vs.\ $+0.0058$ IC in 2026, lifting Sharpe from $1.64 \to 1.97$; Appendix~\ref{app:e10}).

\paragraph{Regime Stress Audit \& Leave-One-Family-Out Ablation.}
In an 80/20 Core-Satellite portfolio (2023--2026 OOS), \texttt{fin-skills} achieves net Sharpe \textbf{1.97} (MDD $-4.41\%$) vs.\ \textbf{0.65} unguarded in CN, and \textbf{1.84} vs.\ \textbf{0.79} in US. Across all four macro regimes---including the 2023 bear grind ($\text{CSI 300 }-11.4\%$, ours $+9.8\%, \text{SR }1.68$) and 2024 micro-cap liquidity crisis ($\text{SR }1.82$, Fig.~\ref{fig:e9_e10}b)---guarded execution remains profitable. Leave-one-out ablation (Table~\ref{tab:e11_component}) shows each family is essential: removing Bayesian KOL calibration drops Sharpe by $-0.85$ ($1.97 \to 1.12$), removing Tier-C predictability filtering drops $-0.51$ ($1.46$), removing $T+1$/QDII guards drops $-0.36$ ($1.61$), and removing signal reconciliation drops $-0.23$ ($1.74$).
