Table~\ref{tab:calibration} proves that static future-year holdout (\texttt{2023}) fails to punish same-session leakage ($22.80$ paper Sharpe vs.\ $-0.58$ live clock replay). Evaluating \texttt{FinGuardBench-60} (\path{run_finguard_bench_60.py}) and our multi-model pilot (Table~\ref{tab:pilot}, Fig.~\ref{fig:overview}a) yields three core insights:
\textbf{(1) Guard-Driven Self-Repair:} Reading \texttt{SKILL.md} (\texttt{Cond B}) lifts compliance from $30.0\%$ ($18/60$) to $70.0\%$ ($42/60, p=1.19\times 10^{-7}$), yet boundary traps persist. Executing \texttt{fin\_skills.api} guards (\texttt{Cond C}) returns structured \texttt{GuardResult} diagnostics that repair $73.3\%$ ($11/15$) of \texttt{Pass@1} failures in one round (\texttt{Pass@2}: $93.3\%$) and $\mathbf{93.3\%}$ ($14/15$) within two rounds (\texttt{Pass@3}: $\mathbf{98.3\%}, p=1.22\times 10^{-4}$ vs.\ \texttt{Pass@1}), cutting $|\Delta\text{Sharpe}|$ from $1.04$ to $0.04$ (and Claude Opus error by $84\%$, $0.19 \to 0.03$).
\textbf{(2) The Compliance Paradox:} Under text-only guidance (\texttt{Cond B}), agents \emph{hallucinate guard citations (\texttt{safe\_\allowbreak asof}, \texttt{contamination\_\allowbreak probe}) without executing them} on $53.3\%$ ($32/60$) of tasks, causing weak models (Claude Haiku) to suffer $11.95$ Sharpe distortion. Runtime provenance gating (\texttt{Cond C}) eliminates fabricated citations ($0.0\%$).
\textbf{(3) Integrity Tools Do Not Manufacture Alpha:} On held-out 2023 live replays, library access yielded 1 win ($+4.44\%$ vs.\ $+3.90\%$), 1 loss ($-0.64\%$ vs.\ $+2.36\%$), and 1 tie ($+0.30\%$ vs.\ $+0.55\%$), confirming that guards prevent false discoveries rather than inventing edge.

\paragraph{The $3.96\times$ Follower Asymmetry \& Bayesian Reversal.}
Table~\ref{tab:kol} and Fig.~\ref{fig:overview}b explain why unweighted financial NLP fails: \texttt{TIER\_\allowbreak CONTRARIAN} accounts ($N=98$, 5-day $\text{IC} = -0.2130$, win rate $28.49\%$) average \textbf{$55{,}988.6$ followers---$3.96\times$ more than \texttt{TIER\_1\_\allowbreak CORE\_\allowbreak ALPHA} researchers ($14{,}121.9$ fans, $\text{IC} = +0.2158$)}. Consequently, unweighted MMAN suffers severe negative correlation across $334{,}946$ samples ($\text{Rank IC} = -0.03184, p = 1.74 \times 10^{-20}$, Table~\ref{tab:mman}). Our \texttt{kol-\allowbreak credibility-\allowbreak registry} skill (conjugate Beta-Binomial shrinkage with contrarian polarity inversion) reverses Rank IC to $+0.01040$ ($p = 0.001, \Delta\text{IC} = +0.04224$), while LLM semantic distillation ($N=31{,}505$, cutting hype-to-hedging by $79.8\%$) doubles RoBERTa Rank IC from $+0.00842$ to $+0.01862$ ($t=3.30$).

\paragraph{Microstructure Gates, Cost \& Horizon Sensitivity.}
Deploying six \texttt{fin-china} execution guards ($T+1$ lockup, 100-share board lots, sell-side stamp duty, QDII premium cap $\le 1.5\%$, cash drag, and Tier-C noise blocking) in an 80/20 Core-Satellite portfolio (2023--2026 OOS) achieves net Sharpe \textbf{1.97} (CAGR $+14.85\%$, Calmar \textbf{3.36}) vs.\ \textbf{0.65} for naive social trading ($363$ trades), cutting max drawdown from $-9.57\%$ to \textbf{$-4.41\%$}. Across $\{5, 10, 20, 30\}\text{ bps}$ round-trip costs, guarded Sharpe holds at $1.98 \to 1.95$ while naive social degrades from $0.68 \to 0.52$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:scaling_replay}b), peaking at the $T+5$ horizon ($72.09\%$ win rate vs.\ $53.49\%$ at $T+1$).
