\begin{abstract}
Large language model (LLM) agents increasingly automate quantitative financial research, yet their reported backtest Sharpe ratios routinely collapse out-of-sample. We demonstrate that autonomous financial agents fail through two orthogonal mechanisms: \textbf{Type~I Mechanical Shortcuts} (same-session post-close joins, pre-training window contamination, survivorship filtering, and unadjusted corporate actions) and \textbf{Type~II Empirical \& Microstructure Illusions} (trusting retail social echo-chambers where high-reach influencers carry negative alpha, and violating $T+1$ settlement or board-lot constraints). To address both, we introduce \textbf{\texttt{fin-skills}}, an open-source library of \textbf{129 source-verified skills}, \textbf{36 executable verification guards} (\texttt{Bundle.check()}), and \textbf{53 MCP agent tools}, paired with an external \textbf{Counterfactual Audit Protocol} that grades agent submissions without using the library to grade itself. Across our expanded 180-case boundary and compound guard benchmark (\textbf{\texttt{FinGuardBench-180}}), our 36 guards achieve \textbf{100\% recall and 100\% specificity ($180/180$)} at $1.89\text{ ms}$ median latency. Across 60 quantitative tasks spanning 10 financial domains (\textbf{\texttt{FinGuardBench-60}}), passive \texttt{SKILL.md} reading raises compliance from $30.0\%$ to $70.0\%$ ($p = 1.19 \times 10^{-7}$) but leaves $53.3\%$ \emph{hallucinated guard citations} (yielding $11.95$ Sharpe distortion on weak models); pairing skills with executable guards drives multi-round self-repair (\textbf{Pass@1 $75.0\% \to$ Pass@2 $93.3\% \to$ Pass@3 $98.3\%$}, McNemar $p = 9.09 \times 10^{-13}$), eliminating fabricated citations and compressing Sharpe reporting error from $1.04$ to $0.04$. Finally, auditing \textbf{1.72M bilingual posts and 1,445 A-share KOL track records} reveals a $3.96\times$ follower paradox that drives unweighted NLP into negative correlation ($\text{Rank IC} = -0.0318$) until reversed by Bayesian credibility gating ($+0.0104$), sustaining a $1.97$ net Sharpe ($-4.41\%$ max drawdown vs.\ $0.65$ naive) across 5--30 bps cost regimes (2023--2026 OOS).
\end{abstract}
