\section{Experiment E1: \texttt{FinGuardBench-180}}
\label{app:e1}
In \path{benchmarks/guard_bench_180.py}, we evaluate all 36 guards across 5 modalities ($36 \times 5 = 180$ cases): \texttt{standard\_\allowbreak compliant}, \texttt{standard\_\allowbreak violation}, \texttt{boundary\_\allowbreak compliant}, \texttt{epsilon\_\allowbreak edge\_\allowbreak violation}, and \texttt{compound\_\allowbreak multi\_\allowbreak trap}. Across all 180 cases (\path{GUARD_BENCH_RESULTS.json}), \texttt{fin-skills} achieves \textbf{100\% recall ($108/108$)} and \textbf{100\% specificity ($72/72$)} at $1.89\text{ ms}$ median latency, plus $36/36$ recall across seeds $\{17,29,43\}$ (\path{GUARD_ROBUSTNESS.json}).

\section{Experiment E2: \texttt{FinGuardBench-60}}
\label{app:e2}
Across 60 tasks spanning all 10 plugin domains (\path{run_finguard_bench_60.py}): \textbf{Cond.\ A} passes $18/60$ ($30.0\%$); \textbf{Cond.\ B} passes $42/60$ ($70.0\%, p=1.19\times 10^{-7}$) with $53.3\%$ hallucinated citations; \textbf{Cond.\ C} progresses from $45/60$ (\texttt{Pass@1}: $75.0\%$) to $56/60$ (\texttt{Pass@2}: $93.3\%$) and $59/60$ (\texttt{Pass@3}: $\mathbf{98.3\%}, p=9.09\times 10^{-13}$ vs.\ A; $0\%$ hallucinated citations).
