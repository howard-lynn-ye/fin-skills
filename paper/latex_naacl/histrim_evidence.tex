\section{Recommendation evidence retained from the source manuscript}
\label{app:histrim_evidence}
This section preserves the substantive HiSTrim comparison from the shared manuscript.
The source is the Overleaf revision uploaded by Shwai He on September 22, 2026, with
the interface displaying 19:05. Its TeX and figures were downloaded before merging.
The tables below are author-reported manuscript values, not an independent replication:
the original training code, user-level predictions and run receipts were not available
in this workspace. The local merge record stores source hashes and arithmetic checks.
This evidence status is separate from the financial result files used elsewhere.

\paragraph{Generative recommendation and baselines.}
Table~\ref{tab:histrim_source_mrr} retains all five task columns and all six method
rows per model scale from the source's principal generative table. Keeping every row
shows where other methods outperform HiSTrim, including the Video task. The table
does not support universal superiority across tasks. Reported KV retention also
differs between methods, so these rows are not perfectly matched resource budgets.

\paragraph{Calibration-seed variability.}
The source appendix lists five calibration partitions for OneRec-1.7B Ad. Recomputing
from its rounded rows yields Pass@32 of $\HSeedPassMean\%\pm\HSeedPassSD\%$ and
MRR@32 multiplied by 100 of $\HSeedMrrMean\pm\HSeedMrrSD$ (mean and sample standard
deviation). The Pass@32 mean resolves the source main text's differing rounded value.
These are variations across fitted routers evaluated on the stated test set, not
independent test populations. Significance claims require the original paired
predictions and sampling procedure and are not inferred from these summary rows.

\paragraph{Task accuracy, agreement and cost.}
Table~\ref{tab:histrim_task_fidelity} retains both favorable comparisons and a
counterexample to treating teacher agreement as task success. On Beauty, the source
reports lower KL for HiSTrim than for TokenDrop-50, while its label MAE is higher.
On the hybrid ranking task, the aggressive variant is faster but has lower NDCG than
the dense baseline. The Goodreads comparison reports lower MAE with reduced KV;
the original prediction records remain necessary to assess uncertainty. These
contrasts motivate joint measurement of quality and resources rather than a single
aggregate claim of improvement.

\paragraph{Items requiring reconciliation.}
The source's figure captions and tables contain different aggregate configurations;
we retain the explicit table rows rather than combining their maxima. The source
also describes several calibration-set sizes without a complete run-to-configuration
map. Hardware timings, significance markers, broad baseline compatibility claims and
the proposed transfer to financial agents require their supporting run records.
The untouched source remains available in the pre-merge backup. These gaps do not
alter the financial study's recorded outcomes and are not filled with new experiments
or assumed results in this merge.
