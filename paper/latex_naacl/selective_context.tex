\section{Selective context with HiSTrim}
\label{sec:histrim}
HiSTrim contributes a concrete mechanism for selecting historical context while
retaining task constraints. The source study uses chronological interaction items
for recommendation. A financial application would instead need to define meaningful
record spans and preserve timestamps, asset identifiers and task requirements. This
mapping is a proposed application; it is not part of the completed financial runs.

\subsection{Protected, sign-preserving selection}
Let $\mathcal{H}_{\ell-1}$ be the historical items surviving before pruning layer
$\ell$, and let $\mathcal{P}$ be a disjoint set of protected token positions. For an
item representation $h_m$, the dual router computes
\begin{align}
 g_m &= w_g^\top h_m+b_g, & u_m &= w_u^\top h_m+b_u,\\
 r_m &= \sigma(g_m)u_m, & \sigma(g)&=(1+e^{-g})^{-1}.
\end{align}
For finite real logits, $\sigma(g_m)>0$, so $\operatorname{sign}(r_m)=
\operatorname{sign}(u_m)$; holding $g_m$ fixed gives
$\partial r_m/\partial u_m=\sigma(g_m)>0$. By contrast,
$\operatorname{SiLU}(g_m)u_m$ is positive when both logits are negative. The sigmoid
form removes this algebraic sign reversal. It does not prove that the utility score
is correct or that thresholding will retain every useful item.

The router standardizes scores within a sequence and applies a learned threshold:
\begin{align}
 z_m &= (r_m-\mu_\ell)/(s_\ell+\epsilon),\\
 \mathcal{H}_\ell &= \{m\in\mathcal{H}_{\ell-1}:z_m\ge\tau_\ell\},\\
 \mathcal{T}_\ell &= \mathcal{P}\;\cup\!\bigcup_{m\in\mathcal{H}_\ell}\mathcal{I}_m.
\end{align}
Here $\mathcal{I}_m$ contains item $m$'s token positions. This separates item indices
from token indices and ensures $\mathcal{H}_\ell\subseteq\mathcal{H}_{\ell-1}$.
Protected positions remain in $\mathcal{T}_\ell$. Their retention prevents literal
deletion, but changes in surrounding history can still change their hidden states
and the model's answer. No equality of mutual information follows from masking alone.

\subsection{Router fitting and physical compaction}
The source method initializes routers from ridge-regression directions fitted on
calibration data, normalizes each direction, and normalizes their weighted average.
For nonzero fitted directions $v_s$, nonnegative weights summing to one, and a nonzero
average, this gives
\begin{equation}
 w_0=\frac{\sum_s\alpha_s v_s/\|v_s\|_2}
 {\left\|\sum_s\alpha_s v_s/\|v_s\|_2\right\|_2}.
\end{equation}
This construction fixes the norm; variance reduction needs additional distributional
assumptions. A zero direction or cancelling average requires explicit handling.
Forward-only refinement evaluates paired perturbations and updates directions in the
tangent space, using $g_\perp=(I-ww^\top)g$ for unit $w$, followed by normalization.
Thresholds are updated separately. These operations describe an optimization procedure,
not a guarantee of convergence or avoidance of threshold collapse. Fixed numerical
stabilizers also limit exact scale invariance of standardized scores.

At inference, HiSTrim-VarLen gathers retained entries into packed buffers with
sequence offsets. Physical gathering distinguishes a smaller cache from a mask that
leaves all entries allocated. Real memory and latency depend on the backend, sequence
lengths, beam sharing, packing overhead and retained fraction; they must be measured
for the model executing the financial task.

\subsection{Attention compensation and its limits}
Eviction changes attention normalization. For a fixed query, let $s_0$ be a sink
logit and let $\mathcal{E}$ contain the removed positions, excluding the sink.
Replacing its exponential mass by the sink plus the removed mass would require
\begin{equation}
 \Delta^*=\log\left(1+\sum_{j\in\mathcal{E}}e^{s_j-s_0}\right).
\label{eq:exact_sink}
\end{equation}
Then $e^{s_0+\Delta^*}=e^{s_0}+\sum_{j\in\mathcal{E}}e^{s_j}$ exactly for these
fixed logits. Even this identity preserves only the denominator: preserving the
attention output also requires the corresponding value-weighted contribution.

The source's practical offset $\beta\log(|\mathcal{E}|+1)$ approximates the missing
mass using only a count. Equality with Equation~\ref{eq:exact_sink} requires a
specific relation between removed logits and the sink; it is not an unconditional
conservation theorem. We therefore retain it as an attention-compensation heuristic
whose effect must be measured, together with protected spans and sign-preserving
routing. These qualifications preserve the method without attributing guarantees
that the stated assumptions do not establish.

\paragraph{Evidence and transfer.}
The recommendation study separates semantic-ID generation, rating prediction and
hybrid ranking. Appendix~\ref{app:histrim_evidence} preserves its reported results
and their provenance. Teacher agreement and label accuracy answer different questions;
neither directly measures financial research correctness. A joint test must compare
the same financial model with and without context selection under matched information
and audit access, rather than transfer a recommendation gain to a return claim.
