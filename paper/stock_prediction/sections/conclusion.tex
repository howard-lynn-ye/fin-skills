\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
\vspace{-0.08cm}
We introduced \textbf{\texttt{fin-skills}} alongside an external counterfactual and microstructure audit methodology for financial AI agents. Combining black-box perturbations, multi-round guard self-repair ($98.3\%$ \texttt{Pass@3} vs.\ $71.7\%$ Python \texttt{Self-Debug}), and bilingual Bayesian KOL calibration ($2{,}521$ KOLs) eliminates backtest shortcut illusions and bridges financial NLP with live execution.

\section*{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
\vspace{-0.14cm}
We note three scope boundaries and extensions:
\begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{0.5pt}\setlength{\parskip}{0pt}\setlength{\topsep}{1.5pt}
    \item \textbf{Bar/Session vs.\ Nanosecond L3 Granularity}: While \texttt{fin-skills} provides comprehensive verification and execution modeling across daily and intraday equity, ETF, and derivatives workflows ($T+1$ settlement, auction windows, board-lot rounding, and Almgren-Chriss impact), our current guard suite does not model nanosecond-level FPGA or Level-3 (L3) full limit-order-book queue dynamics, which remains an avenue for ultra-low-latency extensions.
    \item \textbf{Repair Horizon on Compact Models}: While frontier generalist and reasoning models achieve $100.0\%$ ($60/60$) compliance within three guard-guided repair rounds (\texttt{Pass@3}), compact $8\text{B}$ models (\texttt{Small-Fast Tier}) reach $91.7\%$ ($55/60$) at \texttt{Pass@3} on deeply nested multi-table lineage tasks, suggesting that lightweight local agents benefit from a deeper repair budget (\texttt{Pass@5}) or AST patch templates.
    \item \textbf{Rolling Calibration for Social Platforms}: Although our empirical Bayesian KOL credibility framework yields consistent cross-market gains across $2{,}521$ bilingual accounts on Xueqiu and StockTwits, evolving platform recommendation feeds and contributor cohorts motivate scheduled rolling-window prior updates in continuous production deployments.
\end{enumerate}
