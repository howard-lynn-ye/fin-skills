\begin{abstract}
Large language model (LLM) agents increasingly automate quantitative financial research, yet their reported backtest Sharpe ratios routinely collapse out-of-sample. We demonstrate that autonomous financial agents fail through two orthogonal mechanisms: \textbf{Type~I Mechanical Shortcuts} (same-session post-close joins, pre-training window contamination, survivorship filtering, and unadjusted corporate actions) and \textbf{Type~II Empirical \& Microstructure Illusions} (trusting retail social echo-chambers where high-reach influencers carry negative alpha, and violating $T+1$ settlement or board-lot constraints). To address both, we introduce \textbf{\texttt{fin-skills}}, an open-source library of \textbf{129 source-verified skills}, \textbf{36 executable verification guards} (\texttt{Bundle.check()}), and \textbf{53 MCP agent tools}, paired with an external \textbf{Counterfactual Audit Protocol} that grades agent submissions without using the library to grade itself. Across our 180-case boundary and compound benchmark (\textbf{\texttt{FinGuardBench-180}}), our guards achieve \textbf{100\% accuracy ($180/180$, $0$ false alarms)} at $1.89\text{ ms}$ median latency. Across 60 tasks spanning 10 domains (\textbf{\texttt{FinGuardBench-60}}), passive \texttt{SKILL.md} reading (\texttt{Cond B}) lifts compliance from $30.0\%$ to $70.0\%$ ($p = 1.19 \times 10^{-7}$) but triggers $53.3\%$ \emph{hallucinated guard citations}, while generic 3-round Python \texttt{Self-Debug} (\texttt{Cond B+}) stagnates at $71.7\%$ ($p=1.0$) because $98.3\%$ of financial leaks execute with Python exit code $0$. Pairing skills with executable guards (\texttt{Cond C}) drives multi-round self-repair (\textbf{Pass@1 $75.0\% \to$ Pass@2 $93.3\% \to$ Pass@3 $98.3\%$}, $p = 3.05 \times 10^{-5}$ vs.\ \texttt{B+}) with $0\%$ fabricated citations across four model tiers. Finally, auditing \textbf{1.72M bilingual posts and 2,521 verified KOLs} ($1{,}445$ China Xueqiu, $3.96\times$ follower asymmetry; $1{,}076$ US StockTwits, $3.15\times$ asymmetry) reverses negative NLP correlation in both markets ($\text{Rank IC}: -0.0318 \to +0.0104$ CN; $-0.0218 \to +0.0094$ US) and sustains net Sharpe $1.97$ ($-4.41\%$ max drawdown) across 5--30 bps cost regimes.
\end{abstract}
