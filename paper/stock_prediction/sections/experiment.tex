Table~\ref{tab:calibration} proves that static year holdouts (\texttt{2023}) fail to punish same-session leakage ($22.80$ paper Sharpe vs.\ $-0.58$ live replay). Evaluating \texttt{FinGuardBench-60} (\path{run_finguard_bench_60.py}) across four conditions and four model tiers (Table~\ref{tab:pilot}, Fig.~\ref{fig:overview}a, Appendix~\ref{app:e5}) yields three insights:
\textbf{(1) Python \texttt{Self-Debug} vs.\ Guard Self-Repair:} Reading \texttt{SKILL.md} (\texttt{Cond B}) lifts compliance from $30.0\%$ to $70.0\%$ ($p=1.19\times 10^{-7}$). Adding 3 rounds of Python \texttt{Self-Debug} \citep{chen2023selfdebug} without domain guards (\texttt{Cond B+}) stagnates at $71.7\%$ ($43/60, p=1.0$ vs.\ \texttt{B}): because $59/60$ ($98.3\%$) of financial flaws (e.g., same-day \texttt{pd.merge}) exit with code $0$ and inflate Sharpe, generic feedback only catches $1/18$ failures (\texttt{ValueError}). Conversely, \texttt{fin\_skills.api} guards (\texttt{Cond C}) return structured \texttt{GuardResult} diagnostics that repair $73.3\%$ ($11/15$) of failures in one round (\texttt{Pass@2}: $93.3\%$) and $\mathbf{93.3\%}$ ($14/15$) in two rounds (\texttt{Pass@3}: $\mathbf{98.3\%}, p=3.05\times 10^{-5}$ vs.\ \texttt{B+}), cutting $|\Delta\text{Sharpe}|$ from $1.04 \to 0.04$.
\textbf{(2) Cross-Model Scaling of the Compliance Paradox:} Under \texttt{Cond B}, agents \emph{fabricate citations to guards (\texttt{safe\_\allowbreak asof}) never executed}. Across four model tiers (Fig.~\ref{fig:domain_scaling}b), hallucinated citations scale inversely with capability---from $65.0\%$ on \texttt{Small-Fast} (Haiku/8B, $11.95$ pilot Sharpe gap) and $53.3\%$ on \texttt{Open-Coder} (Qwen-32B) to $28.3\%$ (\texttt{Sonnet/4o}) and $8.3\%$ (\texttt{Opus/R1})---while \texttt{Cond C} reduces hallucinations to $\mathbf{0.0\%}$ and lifts \texttt{Pass@3} to $91.7\%\text{--}100\%$.
\textbf{(3) Eliminating Paper-to-Live Sharpe Distortion:} Stripping out mechanical leaks collapses artificial paper-to-live Sharpe distortion ($|\Delta\text{Sharpe}|: 1.04 \to 0.04$), ensuring that reported backtest performance faithfully tracks true out-of-sample live execution.

\paragraph{Universal Follower Asymmetry \& Bayesian Reversal.}
Table~\ref{tab:kol} and Fig.~\ref{fig:overview}b confirm that Social Megaphone Bias holds across both languages: contrarian indicators command \textbf{$3.96\times$ more followers in China ($55{,}989$ vs.\ $14{,}122$, $\text{IC} = -0.2130$)} and \textbf{$3.15\times$ more in the US ($58{,}120$ vs.\ $18{,}450$, $\text{IC} = -0.1764$)} than Tier-1 Alpha researchers. Consequently, unweighted sentiment suffers negative correlation in both markets ($\text{Rank IC} = -0.03184$ CN, $-0.02184$ US, Table~\ref{tab:mman}). Our \texttt{kol-\allowbreak credibility-\allowbreak registry} skill (Beta-Binomial shrinkage with contrarian polarity inversion) reverses Rank IC to $+0.01040$ (CN, $p = 0.001$) and $+0.00942$ (US, $p = 0.0018$), while LLM distillation ($N=31{,}505$, cutting hype-to-hedging by $79.8\%$) doubles RoBERTa IC ($+0.00842 \to +0.01862$).

\paragraph{Microstructure Gates, Cost \& Horizon Sensitivity.}
Enforcing six \texttt{fin-china} guards ($T+1$ lockup, 100-share lots, sell-side stamp duty, QDII cap $\le 1.5\%$, cash drag, Tier-C blocking) in an 80/20 Core-Satellite portfolio (2023--2026 OOS) achieves net Sharpe \textbf{1.97} (MDD $-4.41\%$) vs.\ \textbf{0.65} (MDD $-9.57\%$) unguarded; US microstructure gates (Reg SHO borrow fees, Form~4 timestamps) lift US Sharpe from \textbf{0.79 to 1.84} (MDD $-13.42\% \to -5.68\%$). Across $\{5, 10, 20, 30\}\text{ bps}$ costs, guarded CN Sharpe holds at $1.98 \to 1.95$ vs.\ naive $0.68 \to 0.52$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:scaling_replay}b), peaking at $T+5$ ($72.09\%$ win rate).
