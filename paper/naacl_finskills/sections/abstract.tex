\begin{abstract}
Large language model (LLM) agents increasingly automate quantitative financial research, yet their reported backtest Sharpe ratios frequently collapse out-of-sample. We show that financial agents fail through two orthogonal mechanisms: \textbf{Type~I Mechanical Shortcuts} (same-session post-close joins, pre-training window contamination, survivorship filtering, and unadjusted corporate actions) and \textbf{Type~II Empirical \& Microstructure Illusions} (trusting retail social echo-chambers where high-reach influencers carry negative alpha, or assuming price-only technical tools generate edge without point-in-time information gating). We present \textbf{\texttt{fin-skills}}, an open-source verification library of \textbf{129 source-verified skills}, \textbf{48 algorithm--backend adapters}, \textbf{36 executable research guards} (\texttt{Bundle.check()}), and \textbf{56 JSON/MCP tools}, paired with an external \textbf{Counterfactual Audit Protocol} and a \textbf{checked delayed-feedback interface} for online memory adaptation. Across 216 stress cases and \DefectInstances{} multi-seed planted defects, our guards achieve \textbf{100\% recall ($0$ false alarms)} and \textbf{\ParityPassed{}/\ParityTotal{} numerical parity}. Evaluating four interlocking experimental pillars, we find: \textbf{(1) Audit \& Self-Repair:} a \PilotRuns{}-run Opus/Haiku pilot (\OpusReduction{}\% Opus error reduction vs.\ Haiku degradation) and a 4-condition Beacon study (\texttt{C0--C3}) expose why open-weight agents fail unguarded or under interface dtype/index bugs; resolving those bottlenecks on \textbf{\texttt{FinGuardBench-60}} shows generic Python \texttt{Self-Debug} (\texttt{Cond B+}) stagnates at $71.7\%$ because $100\%$ ($60/60$) of silent financial leaks exit with Python code $0$, whereas enforced guard feedback (\texttt{Cond C}) drives multi-round repair (\textbf{Pass@1 $75.0\% \to$ Pass@3 $98.3\%$}, $p = 3.05 \times 10^{-5}$). \textbf{(2) Progressive Routing \& Autonomous Use:} progressive skill discovery cuts prompt tokens by \textbf{98.91\%} ($4{,}039$ vs.\ $369{,}315$ tokens, $100\%$ guard recall), enabling Qwen2.5-7B/14B to complete \textbf{64/64 valid portfolio decisions} and \textbf{43/43 error-free tool calls} in an autonomous FX study, while demonstrating that price-only tools alone do not beat passive controls. \textbf{(3) Point-in-Time Credibility Gating:} auditing \textbf{\KOLPredRows{} timestamped predictions} across \textbf{\KOLTotalAccounts{} verified bilingual KOLs} (\KOLCNCount{} China Xueqiu + \KOLUSCount{} US StockTwits across \KOLTradingDates{} trading dates) via \texttt{prediction\_audit.py} reverses mean daily cross-sectional Rank IC from \textbf{\KOLNaiveDailyIC{}} to \textbf{\KOLGatedDailyIC{}} (paired gain \textbf{\KOLPairedDiffIC{}}, 5-day block-bootstrap 95\% CI $[+0.0074, +0.0728]$). \textbf{(4) Checked Delayed Feedback:} coupling complete-episode feedback verification with 60-day rolling online adaptation further lifts mean daily Rank IC to \textbf{\KOLRollingDailyIC{}} across multi-regime market stress.
\end{abstract}
