Notes on the small / negative deltas (all are real defects, all caught above):
  - each sub-signal is 1/5 of the blended composite, so a leak in one (unpurged
    CV, shared scaler, restated fundamentals) barely moves the blended Sharpe
    even as the guard flags it at the source.
  - forward_adjusted_qfq moves price LEVELS only; returns (hence Sharpe) are
    unchanged by construction - a data-integrity defect, not a Sharpe lie.
  - unadjusted_split DEGRADES the Sharpe: the strategy trades the fake -50% split
    jumps and loses. A negative delta is still a corrupted backtest.
  - llm_cutoff_overlap / single_calm_quarter change the WINDOW, not the signal;
    the sign depends on which window, which is exactly why coverage must be
    audited structurally rather than trusted from one number.
